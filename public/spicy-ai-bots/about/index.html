The spice scale

Spicy AI Bots is an independent, just-for-fun ranking of the AI chatbots available on Candy AI.

How the score works

Every bot starts at 1 chili. Bold traits add one chili each: dominant, bratty, seductive, flirty, sexting, forbidden scenarios (stepfamily, cheating), femdom, MILF or cougar energy. Softer traits — shy, sweet, innocent, caring, romantic — remove half a chili. The total is rounded and capped between 1 and 5.

What it means

The level tells you how a bot starts, not how far a conversation can go. You're always in control of the pace.
