Partly. Episode 1 of every series plays without an account and episode 2 with a free account. From episode 3, episodes need Candy Premium and cost tokens (usually 12 tokens, 30 to 50 on some series with well-known creators).

Only on candy.ai: the first episode of each series without an account, the second with a free account. Full episodes on other sites (Dailymotion, "leaks") are unofficial reuploads; we don't link to them.

Candy Shorts is part of Candy AI, which runs in the browser on phone and computer and can be added to your home screen like an app. There is no separate official Candy Shorts app that we know of.
