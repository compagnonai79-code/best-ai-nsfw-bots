Our criteria

20%

Free access (20%)

Unlimited free chat: 10. Confirmed free start: 7. No free option: 2. Not verified: 5.

25%

Price (25%)

Average of two scores: the month-to-month price and the lowest monthly price on a longer plan. 10 points up to 4 €/$ a month, then 0.35 points less per €/$ above that (minimum 2). Price not shown publicly: 5.

35%

Features (35%)

2 base points, then up to 8 points for six features: images, voice messages, calls, video, group chat and a mobile app.

20%

Privacy & payments (20%)

2 base points, +4 if the name on your bank statement is discreet, +2 if crypto is accepted, +1 for Apple Pay or Google Pay, +1 for PayPal.

A fact we could not verify counts half. The final score is the weighted average out of 10, divided by 2 (score out of 5), rounded to one decimal. Ties are broken by the lower price.

Affiliate links never enter the calculation: the formula has no input for them.
