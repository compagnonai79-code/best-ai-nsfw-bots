1. What exactly is Seduced AI?

Seduced presents itself as an adult image and video generator, with no chatbot or virtual girlfriend. Everything is built for visuals: 30+ image models (realistic, semi-realistic, hentai/anime), 300+ themed “extensions”, videos from images, realistic audio and the option to extend a scene.

If you want an AI girlfriend to talk to, this is not the right tool: see our Candy AI review or our AIAllure review instead.

2. Interface and customisation

According to the test, the interface is clear and guided from sign-up, even for beginners. You can create a solo woman or group scenes (with a man, two women, a femboy), then set hair colour and cut (braids, curls, pixie…), breast size, outfits (from lingerie to latex, corset, maid…) and even nationality.

  • ✅ Stackable extensions to specify pose, outfit or setting (2 on Pro, 4 on Platinum, 8 on Diamond).
  • ✅ Saved characters (“AI Pornstars”) to keep the same face from one image to the next.
  • ✅ A dashboard gathering all your creations, to edit or delete.
  • ℹ️ No 180° or 360° VR files: standard images and videos only.

3. Quality and speed

The generator is fast: images come out in seconds and the skin rendering is described as stunning. The limits are typical for this kind of tool:

  • ❌ Anatomy gets less reliable with more than two characters.
  • ❌ Hands often need several attempts.
  • ❌ The longer a video, the more inconsistencies appear.

5. Payment, cancellation and privacy

  • Payment: card (via Epoch or Payblis) or cryptocurrency.
  • Cancellation: for cards via Epoch, under “My Plan” then “Cancel subscription”; access lasts until the end of the paid period. Crypto and Payblis payments are one-off, with no automatic renewal.
  • Privacy: private creations, and Seduced says it does not store card numbers. The source test still advises caution: the site owner's identity is not transparent.
  • Real people: using a real person's face requires that person to be verified.

Verdict

Seduced AI is an effective tool if you want to create highly customised adult images and short videos with reusable characters. It is not an AI companion: there is no conversation at all. And the budget can climb fast with video and options. For video, go straight for Platinum; for images only, Pro is enough.
